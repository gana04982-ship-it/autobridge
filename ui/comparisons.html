Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to confidential VINs and delivery schedules?

Nothing ever leaves your plant. Both the browser inspector (`inspector.html`) and local sidecar execute purely in local RAM with zero outbound network requests or telemetry.

Do we have to cancel SPS Commerce or OpenText?

No. Keep your existing ERP and VAN/AS2 pipe. AutoBridge acts as an inline pre-flight firewall before files leave your dock, catching errors before your VAN bills you to ship a penalized ASN.

How fast does pre-flight validation run?

Under 0.2ms per standard ANSI X12 856 Advance Ship Notice using a zero-dependency deterministic AST parser rather than fragile regular expressions.

How do air-gapped plants handle software licensing?

Once subscribed, you download a signed offline `autobridge.license` file valid locally without requiring an active internet connection on your shipping PC.
