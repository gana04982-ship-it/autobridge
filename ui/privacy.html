Automotive Supply Chain Privacy Policy

AutoBridge Labs (“AutoBridge”, “we”, “us”), an autonomous industrial systems division of Sarkaar Groups, respects and enforces strict data confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information across our web applications, pre-flight tools, and verification engines.

1. Our Core Privacy Principle: Zero-Data Retention

Unlike conventional software platforms, AutoBridge is architected under Privacy by Design (GDPR Art. 25):

  • We never store, log, harvest, or aggregate your ANSI X12 856 Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) files, Advanced Ship Notices (ASNs), bill of lading numbers, purchase orders, or part quantities.
  • All parsing, validation, and clearance certificate generation occur locally inside your client browser using client-side JavaScript.
  • Your proprietary automotive payloads never touch an AutoBridge server or third-party cloud database.

2. Zero Tracking & No Commercial Analytics

We respect operational engineering privacy:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not place advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking cookies on your device.
  • No Ad Pixels: We do not embed Meta Pixel, Google AdSense, or data-broker trackers.
  • No Fingerprinting: We do not profile browser canvas configurations or hardware telemetry.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Web Infrastructure

When you request static web assets (HTML, CSS, JS, images) from our public CDN/hosting infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare), standard HTTP request headers are processed in real-time by the host network for transmission:

  • IP address and standard User-Agent string (for basic DDoS mitigation and content delivery).
  • Referrer headers and timestamp of static resource requests.

These infrastructural connection logs are managed by our hosting network providers in compliance with global security baselines and are never correlated with your local EDI auditing activity.

4. Proprietary Manufacturing Data & Trade Secrets

We recognize that automotive bill-of-material schedules, Just-In-Time (JIT) dock timings, and tier supplier codes constitute mission-critical trade secrets under the Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA). Because our system operates on an isolated client-side execution model, AutoBridge never obtains custody, ownership, or licensing rights over any EDI data you inspect.

5. Global Compliance Alignment (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

  • European Union GDPR: We collect zero personal data from end-users of the Web Inspector. No personal data processing records are generated or stored.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): We do not sell, share, or monetize consumer or enterprise data. We have zero third-party commercial data partnerships.
