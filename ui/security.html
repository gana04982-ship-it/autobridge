Zero-Data Retention • Air-Gapped Client Architecture

Enterprise Security & Cryptographic Guarantee

Automotive supply chain data contains confidential production schedules, part serials, and trade secrets. AutoBridge was engineered from first principles so that your EDI payloads never leave your browser.

01

100% Client-Side In-Memory Execution

The AutoBridge Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) parser executes exclusively inside your local browser JavaScript runtime. When you drag and drop or paste an ANSI X12 856 payload, it is parsed directly in volatile client memory. No HTTP network requests transmit your EDI content to any external server.

02

Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

AutoBridge does not maintain backend databases, Redis queues, or analytical warehouses for EDI files. When you close or refresh your browser tab, the AST, raw segments, and dock clearance states are instantly destroyed. Zero bytes are logged.

03

Local SHA-256 Cryptographic Sealing

Every Dock Clearance Certificate is signed with a deterministic SHA-256 digest computed via the standard W3C Web Crypto API (crypto.subtle.digest). This proves the exact mathematical payload that was audited without exposing any payload contents over a network.

04

Air-Gapped & Offline Capable

Once the AutoBridge web application assets are loaded into your browser cache, you can physically disconnect your workstation from the internet or Wi-Fi. The entire pre-flight engine, syntax evaluator, and certificate generator continue to function normally with zero internet connectivity.

Enterprise Compliance & Regulatory Alignment

AutoBridge Labs operates under the stringent data governance frameworks demanded by automotive OEMs (Ford, General Motors, Stellantis, Honda, Toyota) and Tier-1 suppliers:

  • NIST SP 800-171 & CMMC Alignment: Protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) within non-federal systems through local cryptographic isolation.
  • SOC 2 Type II Confidentiality Principles: By maintaining a Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR) architecture, AutoBridge eliminates data liability at the architectural level. There is no central data store subject to breach, subpoena, or unauthorized exfiltration.
  • ITAR & Defense Logistics: Because processing occurs on the customer's controlled local endpoint, defense contractors and dual-use automotive suppliers can inspect logistics manifests without triggering cross-border data transfer violations.
